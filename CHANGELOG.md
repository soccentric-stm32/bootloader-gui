## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-19

### Added

- BOOT Flasher: PySide6 desktop GUI for the NUCLEO-F767ZI A/B bootloader
- Builds and flashes BOOT over SWD
- Clones, builds, signs and uploads the seven firmware projects through BOOT
- Works against the bootloader checkout given by `BOOTLOADER_DIR`
